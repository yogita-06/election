# Content and asset inventory

Source audited: `https://velvety-kelpie-38382d.netlify.app/`

## Architecture and functionality

- Single-page static campaign site in English, Spanish, and Chinese.
- Anchor navigation: Priorities; About (Her story, Endorsements, Social hub); Get involved (Contact, Mutual aid, Volunteer, Merch); Donate.
- Language selector, responsive navigation, mailto actions, donation amount links, social links, and in-page anchors.
- The source contains no form handler and no Anedot URL. Donation and social links are `#` placeholders and are preserved without invented destinations.
- Original page also includes a browser-local inline editing/export utility. This is an authoring artifact rather than public campaign functionality and is not exposed in the redesigned public interface.
